My broke roommate saved more than me

—

She made less money than me. By April she had $1,500 and I had $200.

I had a part-time job and spent like I earned it. Uber Eats, campus coffee, $14 smoothies. She had less income but one system: $25 a week, auto-save, every Friday. Packed lunch every single day. Every student discount she could find.

By April the numbers told the whole story. It was never about income. It was about what you keep.

That was the semester I started the same auto-save.
