How I Paid Off $8,000 in Student Debt in 18 Months

The exact plan, the numbers, and what I learned doing it.

›

The Situation

$8,000

Canada Student Loans

Interest:Prime + 0% (federal) = ~5.0%
Min payment:$150/month
At minimum:5+ years to pay off
Total interest:$1,100 wasted
›

The Plan

Pay $445/month instead of $150. That's $295 extra.

Where I found $295/month:

$150Cutting expenses — meal prep, cancel unused subscriptions, cheaper phone plan
$145Side income — tutoring, freelance work, weekend shifts

Not glamorous. But it worked.

›

Month-by-Month Strategy

Months 1-6
Focus all extra money on highest-interest portion first (avalanche method). Build the habit.
Months 7-12
Momentum builds — you can see the balance dropping fast. Stay the course.
Months 13-18
Final stretch — increase payments with any bonuses or tax refunds. Sprint to zero.

Consistency beats intensity. Every single month.

What I Learned

1) Automate the payments — don't rely on willpower. Set it and forget it.
2) Track weekly, not monthly — it keeps you motivated seeing the balance drop.
3) Save a small buffer simultaneously ($25/week in a savings app) so unexpected costs don't derail your plan.

Debt payoff without savings = one emergency away from more debt.
