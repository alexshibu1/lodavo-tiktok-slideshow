MONEY BASICS

The 5 Canadian Bank Accounts You Actually Need

Most people have too many — or the wrong ones. Here's the system that works.

›
1

Everyday Chequing

For:Bills, rent, daily spending
Look for:No monthly fee (or get it waived), free e-Transfers, good app

Options: Simplii, Tangerine, or get fees waived at a Big 5 bank with a minimum balance.

This is your home base — all income flows in, all bills flow out.

›
2

High-Interest Savings

For:Emergency fund (3-6 months of expenses)
Rates:4.0-5.0% at EQ Bank, Wealthsimple, Neo Financial

Key rule: Keep this separate from chequing so you don't dip in.

This is your safety net — boring but essential.

›
3

TFSA

For:Medium-term goals + investing
Benefit:All growth is tax-free
2024 room:$7,000

Use for: Stocks, ETFs, GICs, or just a savings account.

This is the #1 priority for most young Canadians. Max it before your RRSP.

›
4

RRSP

For:Retirement + first home (HBP: withdraw $60K for a down payment)
How:Tax deduction now, pay tax on withdrawal later

Priority if: Your income is over $55K or your employer offers RRSP matching.

Free employer match = free money. Always take it.

›
5

Prize-Linked Savings

For:Making saving exciting
How:Every $25 saved = 1 free ticket for the weekly draw
Risk:None — your money stays yours, win or not

Prize-linked savings turn boring saving into something you actually look forward to.
