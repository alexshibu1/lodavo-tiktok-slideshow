How to actually start saving in Canada:
Step 1: Open a TFSA before anything else
You get $7,000 in contribution room every year. It grows tax-free. Most people don't open one until their 30s.
Step 2: Set up a $25 auto-save on payday
The money you don't see, you don't spend. Pick a number that doesn't hurt. Auto-save it before you budget anything else.
Step 3: Use the 50/30/20 rule
• 50% needs — rent, groceries, transit
• 30% wants — eating out, clothes
• 20% savings — before you think about it

Adjust as you go. The split doesn't have to be perfect.
Step 4: Make your savings do more
With prize-linked savings, every $25 you save earns a free draw ticket. Every week. Your money stays in your own bank account. You just get chances to win for saving it.
Start with $25.
Build from there.
