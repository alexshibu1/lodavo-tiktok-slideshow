How to stop living
paycheque to paycheque:
Step 1:
Track every dollar for one month
You can't fix what you can't see. Use an app, a spreadsheet, or a notebook. Just write down where it goes.
Step 2:
Cut 3 subscriptions you forgot about
The average Canadian wastes $167/mo on subscriptions they don't use. Check your bank statement right now. Cancel 3.
Step 3:
Build a $500 emergency buffer first
Not $10K. Not $5K. Just $500.
Enough so one bad week doesn't wreck you.
Then build from there.
Step 4:
Auto-save before you budget the rest
Even $25/week adds up to $1,300 in a year. With prize-linked savings, each $25 earns a free weekly draw ticket. Your savings stay in your own bank.
Small moves.
Real progress.
