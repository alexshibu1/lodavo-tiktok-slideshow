the stuff no one tells you until it's too late

Money at 20
Your TFSA Room Starts at 18
By 20 you already have $13,500 in contribution room. That's space to invest tax-free and most people don't touch it until their 30s.
2
Money at 20
Small Amounts Actually Matter
$50/month is $3,600 in 6 years. Starting small still beats starting never. The habit matters more than the number.
3
Money at 20
Audit Your Subscriptions Monthly
Spotify, iCloud, gym. $15 here and $12 there adds up to hundreds a year you forgot you were spending.
4
Money at 20
Your Credit Score Is Being Built Right Now
One missed payment stays on your report for 6 years. Your 20s credit decisions follow you into your 30s.
5
Money at 20
File Your Taxes Even With Zero Income
The GST/HST credit pays up to $519/year. That's free money the government literally wants to give you, but you have to file to get it.
6
Money at 20
Saving Should Feel Like Progress, Not Punishment
Find something that makes saving worth doing every week. If it feels like a chore, you'll quit by February.
