5
Money Mistakes Almost Every Student Makes
you've probably done at least three
1
Student Money Mistakes
Ignoring Your Chequing Balance
One $45 overdraft fee wipes out a week of coffee savings. Check your balance before you tap, not after the notification.
2
Student Money Mistakes
Skipping Student Discounts
SPC, UNiDAYS, campus deals. You're leaving hundreds on the table every year. Your student card is a discount card too.
3
Student Money Mistakes
Leaving Everything in a 0% Chequing Account
A basic HISA pays you something. 0% pays nothing. Even $500 in a high-interest account is better than $500 sitting still.
4
Student Money Mistakes
Stress Buying After Midterms
That $80 Uber Eats order hits different on your statement. Midterm stress fades in a day. The charge stays all month.
5
Student Money Mistakes
Never Once Checking Where Your Money Goes
You can't fix what you can't see. One look at your last month's statement will tell you more than any budgeting book.
